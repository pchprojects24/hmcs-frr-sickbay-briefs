    00:00

    Pick a length to start. An alarm tone plays at the end, so check your volume and don't rely on it for watch calls.

    Hydration Check

    Quick self-check: tap the colour closest to your urine. Aim for pale yellow.

    Pick a colour to see what it means.

    Vitamins (especially B vitamins), some medicines, and foods like beets can change urine colour. Brown or red urine, or dehydration with dizziness, fainting, or vomiting, needs Sick Bay. See Seasickness.
