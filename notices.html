🍂 Seasonal reminder — respiratory virus season

General health information  •  Fall/winter 2026–27

Colds, flu, and COVID-19 spread fast in close quarters. The Public Health Agency of Canada recommends a flu shot every year for everyone 6 months and older.

  • Ask Sick Bay or the Stadacona immunization nurse about this season's flu and COVID-19 vaccines.
  • Wash or sanitize hands often, especially before meals and after heads visits.
  • Cough or sneeze into your sleeve, not your hand.
  • If you have a fever or feel unwell, report to Sick Bay rather than working through it on a crowded mess deck.

Source: Public Health Agency of Canada: flu (influenza)

Scabies Advisory — posted 23 March 2026, now archived

Posted: 23 March 2026  •  Status: Archived (26 September 2026)

Scabies spreads easily in close-quarter environments. All crew members should review the information below and report any symptoms to sickbay promptly.

What Is Scabies?

Scabies is a contagious skin infestation caused by a microscopic mite (Sarcoptes scabiei). The mites burrow into the outer layer of skin, causing intense itching and a rash. It is not a sign of poor hygiene — it spreads through prolonged direct skin contact and, less commonly, through shared bedding or clothing.

Symptoms to Watch For

  • Intense itching, especially at night or after a hot shower
  • A pimple-like rash, small blisters, or red bumps
  • Thin, irregular burrow lines on the skin (often visible in skin folds)
  • Common sites: between fingers, wrists, elbows, waistline, buttocks, and groin

Symptoms may take 2–6 weeks to appear after first exposure. If you have had scabies before, symptoms can appear much sooner (1–4 days) after re-exposure.

What You Should Do

  • Report to sickbay immediately if you have any of the above symptoms.
  • Do not self-treat — prescription medication (permethrin cream) is required and is available through sickbay.
  • All close contacts must be assessed and treated at the same time, even without symptoms, to prevent reinfection.
  • Wash all worn clothing, bedding, and towels in hot water (≥60°C) and dry on high heat. Items that cannot be washed should be sealed in a bag for at least 72 hours.
  • Avoid sharing towels, clothing, or bedding until cleared by medical staff.

Treatment

Treatment is effective. Permethrin 5% cream is applied to the entire body from neck to toes, left on overnight, then washed off. A second application one week later is usually recommended. Itching may continue for 2–4 weeks after treatment — this is normal and does not mean treatment has failed.

Report to Sickbay

If you have symptoms or have had prolonged skin-to-skin contact with someone who may have scabies, come to sickbay during sick parade or contact medical staff directly. Early treatment protects you and the rest of the crew.
